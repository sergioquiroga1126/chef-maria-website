Meet Chef Maria

From Verona, with the feeling of home.

“My memories are full of kitchen smells, long tables, fresh pasta, and the joy of sharing food.”

Chef Maria brings the warmth of Northern Italian cooking to homes and private events across South Florida. Her food is rooted in family, handmade tradition, seasonal ingredients, and the belief that the best meals create connection.

From the first antipasto to the final spoonful of tiramisù, every detail is prepared with care — elegant enough for a celebration, welcoming enough to feel like home.
